In the age of artificial intelligence, data has become the language of everyday work and research.

Here, you can find and access selected datasets for research, analysis, and practical projects. We periodically curate and publish datasets from open-source and publicly available sources.

### Sustainability in Software Engineering

If you are researching sustainability in software engineering, this dataset provides a useful foundation for exploring research conducted in the field between **2018 and 2026**.

The dataset was compiled from six major academic databases:

- IEEE Xplore
- Scopus
- ScienceDirect
- Web of Science
- Springer
- ACM

Zotero and Rayyan were used to organize, screen, evaluate, and select the relevant studies.

The official dataset contains information on **59 selected studies** included in an academic **Systematic Mapping Study (SMS)**.

For access to the **full dataset containing 164 selected studies**, or the **raw data containing 4,266 records in RIS format**, please contact us.
