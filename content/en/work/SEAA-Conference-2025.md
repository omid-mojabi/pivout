Euromicro SEAA (Software Engineering and Advanced Applications) is a long-standing international conference series bringing together researchers, practitioners, and students to exchange research, experience, and emerging developments in software engineering and software-intensive systems. Founded under the Euromicro organization established in 1974, SEAA has reached 52 editions and has been hosted across numerous European and international locations.


## Conference Tracks:

- **DSD (Digital System Design):** Focuses on the design, architecture, and implementation of digital and hardware-oriented systems, covering areas such as embedded systems, hardware/software co-design, digital architectures, FPGA, and related technologies.

- **SEAA (Software Engineering and Advanced Applications):** Focuses on software engineering and its advanced applications, covering areas such as software development methods, Agile practices, software processes, empirical software engineering, software quality, AI-enabled software engineering, and emerging software technologies.
